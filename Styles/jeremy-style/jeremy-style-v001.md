**Invoke:** “Use Jeremy Style.” [Open the reusable skill](../../skills/jeremy-style/SKILL.md).

## The look in one paragraph

Cinematic toy-like 3D character storytelling: oversized pale spherical heads, minimal graphic expressions, tiny rounded clothing/limbs and recognizable simple sets. Use spatial closeups, two-shots, occlusion and scale-revealing wide shots with soft purple/warm lighting. Gold/cream serif labels and compact purple explanation boards clarify the story; pink lines/counters make otherwise invisible relationships visible. Acting and changes of information drive cuts; reaction holds and quiet scenes matter as much as large crowds.

## Dimensional classification

The observed look is **predominantly volumetric real-3D-style characters and environments, with 2D/2.5D explanatory graphics and some flat-looking crowd elements**. The analysis does not reveal the source author's exact rendering software, meshes or rig. A layered PNG/CSS adaptation must be called 2.5D and cannot claim free camera/head turns. Real geometry, camera, light and occlusion are needed for faithful moving spatial scenes.
