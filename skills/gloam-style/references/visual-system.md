# Visual system

Read [source evidence](source-analysis.md) for G01–G12. **Observed** means visible in those samples; every exact number below is a **proposed adaptation default**, not a measurement of the source. Scale for the actual output; inspect the encoded result.

## Core grammar

Cinematic investigation, not generic neon cyberpunk: alternate spacious light analytical boards with dark spatial reconstructions and short dark statistic/question cards. Serif explanations + mono-like metadata + thin rule lines carry an editorial/dossier identity across otherwise different media. Return to one metaphor set so a viewer recognizes the same system in setup, compromise and aftermath. Preserve important uncertainty and sources as part of the composition.

## Palette by role

Observed families: near-black/charcoal, warm off-white paper, cool gray/blue boards and steel, muted blood-red annotation, amber/gold attention/time and restrained blue comparison. These are not sampled official brand hex values.

Proposed project tokens:
- stageDark #0B0E0D; ink #171C1C; paper #E7E3D3; lightBoard #CED3D5.
- danger #B32D2A; clock #E5A925; comparison #416D86; mutedInk #58605D; coin #B58C39.
- White-to-black contrast for main copy; use more saturated/lighter red on dark if needed. Do not put small dark-red labels directly on black or low-contrast gray on cream merely because a source frame does.
- Choose dark OR light reading field per beat; keep red for one important status/annotation, amber for clock/active selection. Blue marks an alternative category, analysis or normal state. Do not encode meaning by hue alone: include label, icon, line style or state.
- Gold is a material accent, not a requirement that every subject be coins. Replace money with an original domain-relevant object when appropriate.

## Typography

Observed: narrow, high-contrast editorial serif headlines/figures; italic serif emphasis/second line; small widely tracked uppercase mono/typewriter-like labels; utility sans within imported news/UI only. Exact font names are unknown.

Proposed: a locally installed/licensed condensed editorial serif (or documented Georgia fallback for a rough proof), a licensed italic companion, and a locally available mono face such as Consolas. Font substitution needs line-break/weight review. Do not silently download fonts or imply a replacement is the source font.

At 1920×1080, start with hero 100–150px, heading 66–88px, statement/quote 42–58px, explanatory label 28–36px, source 22–26px. Source microtype can be visible atmosphere in the reference, but actionable/user-facing text must remain readable at target size. Reduce wording rather than crush it into 14px. Use tracking 0.08–0.18em for short metadata, not for paragraphs. Align financial/metric values by numeric edge; use tabular numerals for timers if the chosen font supports them. Cap main headline at two lines; italic is contrast, not a whole-screen default.

## Composition and layout families

Proposed 1080p baseline: 112px left/right and 80px top/bottom safe margins; 8px unit, 24/40/64px gaps; one dominant reading area. Actual platform UI can require more. Top-left chapter eyebrow and source line should share a grid, not float arbitrarily.

1. **Evidence slab:** card centered or slightly off-center, 60–76% frame width, headline/quote at top, thin rule, structured data below. Offset shadow and 1–3° tilt establish paper without impairing reading (G03/G08/G10). Use new authentic documents or clearly marked illustrative layouts, never fake source screenshots.
2. **Mechanism:** three horizontal anchors (input, authority/action, result); one labeled connector at a time. On vertical output redesign into a column; do not crop the wide scene (G07).
3. **Chronology:** short calendar sequence or time card, then ledger/ticks; state time zone on the first relevant shot and whenever it changes. A countdown/sweep is a visual metaphor unless actual elapsed time is used (G02/G05/G09).
4. **Status row:** three to five numbered cards; muted inactive cards, one amber active card. Add a non-color active marker. Preserve the row between adjacent beats (G04/G05/G09).
5. **Hero statistic/question:** one very large serif phrase, one short qualifier, optional tiny provenance eyebrow; ample negative space (G09/G11/G12). Not a paragraph in giant type.
6. **Case folder:** cream tabbed file, anonymous portrait/silhouette at left, known identity/unknown status at right. Confidence and attribution remain visible (G10). Do not imply a real employee committed a crime with invented portrait/label.
7. **Spatial metaphor:** one room/assembly with a simple reading direction, silhouette separation and a clear action. Compose around geometry and camera, not random object spin (G02/G06).
8. **Framed proof:** crop to relevant excerpt, keep enough context/provenance to prevent distortion. Current user directive forbids recorded screen footage filling the whole frame unless explicitly requested. Use a framed region in the composition even though some reference news inserts are full-frame. Protect private screen areas.

## Material and light continuity

Observed: cool brushed/industrial-looking metal, dark polished plinths/floors, warm metallic coins/cash register, soft pale paper; bright strip lights and selective red/amber practical lights in spatial shots (G02/G04/G06). Not every scene is glossy. Paper remains matte and readable; lettering stays separate from specular highlights.

Proposed defaults: matte paper, low-frequency texture at low strength; shadow mainly below/right with a soft 16–28px blur; steel moderate roughness; dark plinth slightly reflective; red practical light for changed/compromised state; cool key and subtle fill separate the figure/door. Exact metalness/roughness values belong to the chosen renderer and are not interchangeable with source measurements. In flat boards use graphical shadows, not a gratuitous volumetric fog treatment.
